## The premise

Operational work often fails between tools rather than inside them. Relay explores how one shared view could make ownership and momentum visible without creating another noisy dashboard.

## The design question

How might a team understand what needs attention, who owns it, and what changed—without reading a wall of status updates?

<ProjectArtifact variant="relay" caption="A concept artifact exploring priority, ownership, and handoff states." />

## Decisions over decoration

The concept reduces each handoff to three pieces of information: present state, accountable owner, and next meaningful action. Secondary detail stays available, but it no longer competes with the decision in front of the user.

<Decision title="Frame the queue around attention">
Items are ordered by what requires a decision, rather than by when they were created.
</Decision>

<Decision title="Make ownership unmistakable">
Every state change has a visible owner and a clear path to reassignment.
</Decision>

## What this study demonstrates

Relay is concept work, not a shipped client product. It demonstrates workflow framing, information hierarchy, state design, and the translation of an ambiguous operations problem into a coherent product direction.
